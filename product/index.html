FAQ

Fair questions.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. The editor runs entirely in your browser. When you use AI review, the PDF's text — not the file — is sent to Anthropic or OpenAI using your own key.

Where are my API keys stored?

Only in your browser on your device. They are sent only to Anthropic or OpenAI, and "Forget keys" removes them.

Will my comments show up in Acrobat or Preview?

Yes. Saving writes standard PDF annotations — highlights, ink, notes, shapes, stamps and attachments — that other PDF readers display and edit.

Which pens and tablets work?

Any pen that works in a modern browser: Apple Pencil on iPad, Surface Pen, Wacom and XP-Pen tablets, and Android styluses. Pressure works where the browser reports it.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, though a tablet or laptop is more comfortable. Two fingers pan and zoom; the side panel slides up from the bottom.

What about scanned PDFs?

You can mark them up fully. AI review and search need selectable text, so image-only scans won't give the AI anything to read yet.
